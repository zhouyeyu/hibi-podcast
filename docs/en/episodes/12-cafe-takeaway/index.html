Coffee to go, one clear request

Welcome to Hibi, Japanese with Two Bears. You have chosen your coffee and are ready to head out. How do you tell the staff member that you want it to go? Today, keep the order simple and practice just that choice. Our bears start with the useful request.

Listen at the counter

持ち帰りでお願いします。

かしこまりました。

ありがとうございます。

Your turn · 2 seconds

The customer asks for takeaway, the staff member acknowledges her choice, and she says thank you. Was the first line choosing a drink, or explaining that the order should be taken away? Listen again with that purpose in mind.

持ち帰りでお願いします。

Understand the choice and response

In this scene, the drink has already been chosen. The customer does not repeat the whole order. She adds the information the staff member needs next: she wants to take it away. Her first line states that purpose directly.

持ち帰りでお願いします。

The staff member gives a short acknowledgment. That lets the customer know her request has been understood. There is no need to repeat it immediately. We are following a fictional exchange, rather than explaining a particular shop’s packaging policy or every step of ordering.

かしこまりました。

ありがとうございます。

One way to say it is to go

持ち帰りでお願いします。

This means, to go, please. Keep the takeaway choice and the polite request together. Say it clearly and finish naturally. Use it while placing an order or when the staff member is checking how you would like to receive it.

持ち帰りでお願いします。

Change the choice, then the setting

If you decide to stay and have your order inside, use this version.

店内でお願いします。

For here, please. You are still explaining a choice. The first part changes, while the polite way of making the request remains familiar.

Now enter a bakery. You have chosen a piece of bread and want to take it away. Name the item you are referring to.

このパン、持ち帰りでお願いします。

This bread, to go, please. Adding the item makes the request specific, but the purpose is unchanged. Listen to these two variations without adding quantities, sizes, or an entire list of new details today.

Choose without reading

You have ordered a coffee and need to leave soon. The staff member is waiting to hear how you want your order. Without looking at the words, tell them that it is to go.

Your turn · 5 seconds

持ち帰りでお願いします。

Check that you explained the takeaway choice, rather than ordering another drink. That one clear decision is enough. A useful request does not become better just because you add more words.

Change your mind. You have time to sit down and enjoy the coffee inside. Tell the staff member your new choice.

Your turn · 5 seconds

店内でお願いします。

If the two versions get mixed up, return to the picture: are you walking out, or sitting down? Decide what you want to do, then choose the matching request.

Listen again and take it with you

Listen to our bears once more. Notice how the staff member acknowledges the request before the customer thanks them.

持ち帰りでお願いします。

かしこまりました。

ありがとうございます。

Your turn · 2 seconds

Here is today’s word: takeaway. Listen, then picture yourself heading out with your coffee.

持ち帰り

Next time you want an order to go, try saying this one request naturally. Making a simple choice clear is already a real conversation. This is Hibi, Japanese with Two Bears. See you in the next everyday moment.
