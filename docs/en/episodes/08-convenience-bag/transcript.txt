Welcome to Hibi, Japanese with Two Bears. You have chosen a rice ball and reached the checkout. You brought your own shopping bag. When the staff member offers another bag, how can you say you do not need it? Today, practice one clear, polite answer for that small everyday moment.

レジ袋はご利用ですか。

袋はいりません。

かしこまりました。

The scarf-wearing bear is the staff member. The bear in blue is the customer. She asks about a shopping bag, he says he does not need one, and she acknowledges his answer. Which sentence turns down the bag? Listen again.

袋はいりません。

The staff member asks whether you need a shopping bag. Other staff members may ask differently. Notice the item being offered and answer clearly.

レジ袋はご利用ですか。

If you brought your own bag, you can answer like this.

袋はいりません。

The customer does not need a bag. He is not cancelling the purchase. Keep the answer calm, without stretching the ending.

かしこまりました。

The staff member acknowledges the answer, and the exchange is complete. Our goal is to communicate that no bag is needed. A long explanation is unnecessary.

袋はいりません。

The first part names the bag. The rest says it is not needed. Learn the whole sentence together, so you can answer comfortably at the checkout.

袋はいりません。

To make the exchange warmer, add a brief thank you. It acknowledges the help without changing your answer. Listen to the complete version.

袋はいりません。ありがとうございます。

You can finish clearly and let the staff member continue. There is no need to stretch the ending or apologize several times for declining an item.

The same answer can turn down another item you do not need. First, replace the bag with a straw.

ストローはいりません。

This means you do not need a straw. Imagine the staff member is about to give you one. The purpose stays the same, and only the item changes.

Now change the item to chopsticks.

お箸はいりません。

This means you do not need chopsticks. The purpose stays the same; only the item changes. Return to the answer about a bag when you need it.

Say that you do not need a straw.

Finally, say that you do not need chopsticks.

お箸はいりません。

You are buying a rice ball, and your own shopping bag is beside you. The staff member asks if you need a bag. Answer now, without looking at the words.

That short answer is clear. You can also add a thank you.

Check your meaning: you do not need the item. Then practice the rhythm, changing one item at a time.
