Find the milk in a new supermarket

Welcome to Hibi, Japanese with Two Bears. You want to buy milk in an unfamiliar supermarket, but you cannot see the chilled section. Today, name the thing you need and ask where it is. Listen to a short exchange between a customer and a staff member.

Listen to the short scene

牛乳はどこにありますか。

奥の冷蔵庫にあります。

ありがとうございます。

Your turn · 2 seconds

The customer asks where the milk is. The staff member points her toward a refrigerator farther inside the store, and she says thank you. Was her question about the location, or about whether milk was available? Listen to the opening again.

牛乳はどこにありますか。

Follow the purpose of each line

The customer knows what she wants. The missing information is its location. Naming the milk first gives the staff member a clear subject, without requiring a long explanation of everything on her shopping list.

牛乳はどこにありますか。

The reply tells her that it is in a refrigerator farther inside. You do not need to repeat the whole direction immediately. First, connect the answer with the direction and the refrigerated section you can see. The exact arrangement belongs to our fictional store; other shops will have different layouts.

奥の冷蔵庫にあります。

ありがとうございます。

One question about location

牛乳はどこにありますか。

This means, where is the milk? Name the thing you are looking for, then keep the rest of the question together. Listen to the whole rhythm instead of giving every sound its own separate beat.

牛乳はどこにありますか。

Picture yourself in the aisle with a staff member nearby. Ask clearly, then stop and listen. Today, stay with location. Questions about stock, prices, and payment can wait for another moment. You do not need an entire shopping conversation to use this one useful line.

Two small changes

Use the same question to find bread. Listen for the item that changes at the beginning.

パンはどこにありますか。

Where is the bread? You are asking for its location, rather than asking the staff member to bring you a portion.

Now look for a shopping basket. In this supermarket setting, that is the kind of basket you mean.

かごはどこにありますか。

Both versions serve the same purpose. Choose the item you actually need, and practice that version first. Learning the pattern does not require memorizing every product in the shop today.

Speak from the situation

You enter the supermarket with milk at the top of your shopping list. You see ordinary shelves, but no chilled section. A staff member is nearby. Without reading, ask where the milk is now.

Your turn · 5 seconds

牛乳はどこにありますか。

Check that you named the item and asked about its location. If you could not remember everything, listen and try again. There is no need to race the recording.

Change the situation. You want a shopping basket, but cannot find one. Ask using the same question.

Your turn · 5 seconds

かごはどこにありますか。

After your question, let the other person speak. Leaving room for their answer is part of using the sentence in a real conversation.

Listen again and take one word
