すみません、駅はどこですか？

The opening politely gets the other person’s attention. Then she asks where the station is. She is asking about its location, not asking what a station means. This complete question is useful when speaking to someone you do not know. Ask clearly, then leave room for the person to answer.

この道をまっすぐ行ってください。

The reply means, please go straight along this road. The speaker identifies the road, then tells her how to continue. Imagine moving forward along it. The sentence does not tell us how many minutes to walk, or whether the station is on the right. Listen to what is actually given.

ありがとうございます。

Her final line is a thank-you. It acknowledges the help she has received. If you do not understand a direction, you can ask for clarification before finishing the conversation. You do not have to pretend everything is clear just because you have reached the last line of a practice dialogue.
