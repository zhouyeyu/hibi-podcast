A question at the restaurant

Welcome to Hibi, Japanese with Two Bears. You are in an unfamiliar restaurant, looking at a menu. Before choosing a dish, how can you ask for a recommendation? Today, take one useful question into that moment.

Listen to the whole scene

すみません、おすすめは何ですか。

今日はカレーがおすすめです。

じゃあ、カレーにします。

Your turn · 2 seconds

Our scarf-wearing bear has not chosen yet. She asks for a recommendation, hears that curry is recommended today, and then makes her choice. Which sentence asks for the recommendation? Listen to the opening again.

すみません、おすすめは何ですか。

Understand the exchange

She politely gets the staff member’s attention, then asks what they recommend. She does not need to know every dish on the menu. After asking, she pauses and lets the other person answer.

今日はカレーがおすすめです。

The staff member recommends curry today. A recommendation is a suggestion in this situation. It does not mean the dish is the most expensive, or a promise that everyone will like it.

じゃあ、カレーにします。

She decides to have the curry. Notice the order: ask, listen, then choose. You can understand that final response without learning another pattern today. Our main goal is the question at the beginning.

One useful question

おすすめは何ですか。

This means, what do you recommend? Treat it as one complete question. Listen to its rhythm rather than giving every sound a separate beat.

おすすめは何ですか。

Picture the menu in front of you and a staff member nearby. Ask clearly, then leave space for an answer. That small pause turns a memorized line into the beginning of a conversation.

Two small changes

You can narrow the question to a category. First, ask about dessert.

おすすめのデザートは何ですか。

What dessert do you recommend? The purpose is the same; only the range of possible answers is smaller.

Now ask about drinks.

おすすめの飲み物は何ですか。

What drink do you recommend? Do not try to memorize an entire menu now. If these longer versions feel difficult, begin with the short, general question. You can use it before choosing a category.

Answer from the situation

You have just sat down and have not chosen your food. A staff member comes to your table. Ask for a recommendation without looking at the words.

Your turn · 5 seconds

おすすめは何ですか。

Including the polite opening works here too. The important thing is to ask, and give the other person time to respond.

One more situation. You have finished your main dish and want a dessert recommendation. Ask your question now.

Your turn · 5 seconds

おすすめのデザートは何ですか。

You do not need to copy the voice perfectly. First, make your purpose clear: you are asking for a recommendation, rather than a price or an order.

Listen again and take it with you

Return to the restaurant. Listen for the question, the suggestion, and the choice.

すみません、おすすめは何ですか。

今日はカレーがおすすめです。

じゃあ、カレーにします。

Your turn · 2 seconds

When you have not decided, ask first and listen. Here is today’s word.

おすすめ
